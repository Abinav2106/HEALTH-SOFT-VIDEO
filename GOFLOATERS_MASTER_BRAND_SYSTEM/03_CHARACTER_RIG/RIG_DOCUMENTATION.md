# GoFloaters character and aircraft rig

**Implementation:** layered SVG + JavaScript, with no flattened images. All coordinates are in source pixels of the 2000×667 master.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `rig_source/gofloaters-rig-data.js` | Traced colour plates, part clip regions, pivots, eye/mouth geometry, limb centre-lines, hidden-extension patches. Generated by `tools/build_rig_data.py` from `tools/rig_parts.py`. |
| `rig_source/gofloaters-rig.js` | Builds the rig DOM and applies controls (`GoFloatersRig.create(el).set({...})`) |
| `rig_source/gofloaters-freebody.js` | Constructed full-body view: stand, walk, run, jump, upright, lying. **Proposed extension.** |
| `rig_source/gofloaters-motion.js` | Expressions, actions, hero and brand animations as functions of time (`GFMotion.frame(name, t)`) |
| `rig_source/stage.html` | Headless render stage used by the export tools |
| `rig_controls/rig_test_scene.html` | Functional test scene: a slider for every control, plays any animation, dumps state JSON |
| `rig_controls/controls.json` | Control list with recommended working ranges |
| `rig_preview/rig_proof_playback.mp4` | First proof: blink, eyes, head tilt, ears, paw lift; independent aircraft and propeller; dog-only, aircraft-only and full-logo modes |

## How parts are built
* **Traced pieces.** The head, ears, torso, aircraft parts, propeller and seat are clipped copies of the traced plates inside their own transform groups. At rest they render exactly as the master.
* **Redrawn tubes.** The near and far forelegs and the tail are smoothed centre-lines with a per-point interior half-width, fitted to the source by medial-axis analysis (`tools/fit_tubes.py`). The outline is offset by r + stroke/2, so it sits on the source line. Bends are computed on the centre-line, so joints never open.
* **Procedural pieces.** The neck is two cubic bridges between the rotating head and the static torso, so head turns never tear the neck. The eye has white, pupil, upper and lower lids and a happy-eye arc, clipped to the traced eye interior. The mouth switches from the traced smile to procedural shapes (smile, open, O, tongue) whenever it leaves neutral.
* **Hidden extensions.** Every reconstructed hidden area is a separate group with `class="gf-reconstruction"`: neck, ear roots, chest behind the leg, aircraft surface behind the leg, empty cockpit, lower body, wing underside, fuselage line.

## Known limits (honest notes)
* The ears are rigid traced pieces. Beyond about ±5° the near ear's lower edge separates from the neck line, so ear follow-through is authored within ±5°.
* In the rest pose the redrawn limbs and tail differ slightly from the traced master (mean difference 0.45/255 over the whole lock-up). Every brand sequence locks off to the traced master (`masterLock`) in its final frames.
* The full-body view is a construction in the source's vocabulary. A frontal or three-quarter view has **not** been made.
