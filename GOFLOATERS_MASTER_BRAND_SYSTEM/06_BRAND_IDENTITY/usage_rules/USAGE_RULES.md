# Logo usage rules

**Original assets:** full lock-up, dog, aircraft, wordmark, tagline, speed lines (vector reconstructions).
**Proposed:** everything else below is marked proposed.

1. **Master lock-up:** use `02_MASTER_IDENTITY/original_lockup/gofloaters_lockup_full_colour.svg` wherever space allows.
2. **Clear space:** X = height of the "o" in *Go* (125 px at the 2000 px master, about 18.7% of lock-up height). Keep at least 1X clear on every side.
3. **Minimum size (proposed):** lock-up 160 px or 40 mm wide; symbol 48 px or 12 mm; mascot alone 32 px or 8 mm. Below the symbol minimum, use the symbol without speed lines.
4. **Grounds:** white, Cloud or the tints for full colour. On Deep Sky or photography, use the keyline variant (B). On turquoise, use variant C.
5. **Separated elements:** the dog-only and aircraft-only artwork are brand elements, not replacement corporate logos. Their reconstructed hidden areas are shown in the guidelines on p.7.
6. **Do not:** stretch, recolour the dog or aircraft, rotate the lock-up, add shadows or effects, place on low contrast, or re-set the wordmark in another typeface. See `clear_space_minimum_size_misuse.png`.
7. **Composition:** headline on the left, aircraft flying into open space; the mascot looks or points at the message; keep at least 40% negative space. See `composition_principles.png`.
